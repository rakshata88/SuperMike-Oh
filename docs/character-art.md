# Character artwork

The game uses `assets/characters/mike-xiaboo-atlas.png`, a transparent 1774 × 887 RGBA PNG produced with the built-in image generation tool from the user's supplied character sheets. It contains six poses each for Normal Mike, Super Mike, and Prince Xiaboo. `src/characters.js` defines the inspected frame rectangles, foot anchors, scaling, and animation selection.

Normal Mike follows the compact black-T-shirt design in `10_46_24 PM.png`. Super Mike follows the muscular black sleeveless tank-top design in `10_47_38 PM.png`. Prince Xiaboo follows `10_47_28 PM.png`, including cream fur, mismatched eyes, crown, cape, and medallion. The other sheet labeled “Normal Mike” depicts a muscular tank-top form; the T-shirt sheet is used for Normal Mike to preserve the two gameplay states from the brief.

These are reference-based illustrated adaptations for gameplay, not pixel-identical extractions of the original posters. Run poses use a short two-frame cycle; jumps, crouching/punching, and victory use separate poses. The atlas is bundled in production and loaded before the main menu. Existing basic canvas art is retained only as a loading/error fallback. Game collisions and movement rules are unchanged.

## Generation prompt

Use case: identity-preserve.
Asset type: Production transparent game character sprite atlas for SUPER MIKE-OH.
Input references: Image 1 is NORMAL MIKE: use its large black T-SHIRT chibi/full-body illustration and face. Image 2 is SUPER MIKE: use its muscular black sleeveless TANK TOP hero, same face and hairstyle. Image 3 is PRINCE XIABOO: use its cream-white cat, brown left eye and blue-gray right eye, crown with red jewel, red cape with cream spotted fur trim, gold paw medallion.
Primary request: Adapt these EXACT illustrated character designs into one clean high-detail illustrated sprite atlas, preserving recognizable facial identity and clothing. This is NOT a reinterpretation into geometric icons or generic people. Match the expressive polished inked and shaded cartoon style in the supplied sheets, same tan skin, textured tousled black hair, eyebrows, moustache and little goatee. Normal Mike is compact and slim in black short-sleeve T-shirt, charcoal pants, black canvas sneakers with white soles. Super Mike is wider and taller with big defined arms and shoulders and BLACK SLEEVELESS TANK TOP, charcoal cargo joggers and black athletic shoes with white soles.
Layout: EXACTLY 6 equal columns by 3 equal rows, 18 individual isolated full-body characters, on a genuinely transparent alpha background. Landscape 3072x1536 if available. NO text, labels, grid lines, shadows, ground, scenery, decorative elements or additional characters. Every complete character entirely inside its own cell with generous transparent padding so cells can be sliced cleanly. Put each character's foot baseline at 90% of its cell height and head around 10%; consistent framing and body scale across each row. No feet/ears/capes cropped.
ROW 1: Normal Mike, 6 columns left to right: (1) relaxed confident standing idle, three-quarter facing right with recognizable face; (2) running right, left leg forward; (3) running right, right leg forward; (4) jumping right with one fist raised; (5) crouching ready to land; (6) smiling victory with both fists raised.
ROW 2: Super Mike, matching poses: (1) standing confident muscular idle; (2) running right left leg forward; (3) running right right leg forward; (4) jumping right one fist raised; (5) power punch extended to the right with no flame or effects; (6) happy double-biceps flex victory. Face MUST stay the same Mike.
ROW 3: Prince Xiaboo, 6 columns: (1) sitting looking at viewer, calm with heterochromia; (2) running right forelegs extended; (3) running right legs gathered; (4) leaping right with cape trailing; (5) curled up asleep with crown and cape; (6) sitting happily eyes closed smiling. Cat consistently cream-white, round sweet face, gold crown, red royal cape, gold collar.
Preserve detailed character likeness from the references. Transparent backgrounds including between legs, under arms and around tails. No checkered background printed into the image. No words anywhere.

Preserve ALL 18 character designs, expressions, poses, faces, outlines, colors, scale, positions, and canvas dimensions exactly. Change ONLY the gray checkerboard background: remove it completely and replace it with a truly transparent alpha channel, including spaces between arms and body, legs, and around tails. Do not draw a checkerboard pattern to represent transparency. Output actual RGBA PNG with zero alpha in empty spaces. Do not add any shadows, grid lines, labels, or other elements. Keep Normal Mike's black T-shirt, muscular Super Mike's black sleeveless tank, and Prince Xiaboo's cream fur, heterochromia, red cape, gold crown and medallion unaltered.
